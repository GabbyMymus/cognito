Interference Match

A short memory study (about 5 minutes).

What you will do

You will see small sets of words or shapes for a moment. After a short wait, sometimes with a quick side task, you will pick out the items you saw.

Your data

We record your age, gender, course, email and your answers. The data is used only for a class project in Cognitive Psychology and is not shared outside the group. You can stop at any time by closing this page.

About you

Please fill in every field (age 10 to 99, valid email).

How it works

  1. Look at the + in the middle.
  2. 5 items appear (words or shapes). Try to remember all of them.
  3. They disappear. During the wait you may get a quick side task:
    • Rhyme? Do the two words rhyme? Press F = yes, J = no (or tap the buttons).
    • Same shape? Is the right shape the same as the left one, just turned? Press F = same, J = different (mirror image).
    • Sometimes there is no side task. Just wait.
    Answer the side task quickly and accurately.
  4. Then you see 10 items. Click the 5 you saw, then press Done.

First you get 2 practice rounds with feedback.

Practice done

Now the real task: 24 rounds, about 4 minutes. There is no feedback from now on.
